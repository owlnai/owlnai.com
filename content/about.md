Online, I'm owlnai. By day, I'm a full-stack developer at MRM, where I lead projects, help shape their architecture and mentor the people around me.

I got into web development as a teenager, building simple websites in Notepad and editing wikis, and found that code could make pages come alive.

I went deep into UI design because of a creative friend who has since passed away.

I want design to be recognizable and to last. Above all, it should be accessible: if someone can't use it, it hasn't done its job.
