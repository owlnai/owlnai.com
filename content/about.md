Online, I'm owlnai. By day, I'm a full-stack developer at MRM, building for some of the world's most recognizable brands. In practice that means leading projects, helping shape their architecture and mentoring the people around me.

As a teenager, I built simple websites using HTML in Notepad. My love for wikis and sharing knowledge led me to web development, where I discovered that code could make pages come alive.

Inspired by a creative friend who passed away, I went deep into UI design and learned that great design should be recognizable and timeless. Most of all, it should be accessible: a bridge between what people need and the system in front of them.

These days, I do a little bit of everything. Besides MRM, I run sector9, my web design studio, and I'm building Songbird Labs, Roblox tools that empower creators. I believe everyone should be kind and give back to the community.
